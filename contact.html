Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers about authentication, shipping, and sizing

How are the sneakers authenticated?
Every single sneaker undergoes a multi-stage authentication process by our experts. We inspect everything from stitching and glue lines to label fonts and box packaging to guarantee 100% authenticity.
What is your shipping and delivery timeframe?
Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days. Express shipping is available for 1–2 business days. Tracking details will be emailed immediately once your order is dispatched.
Do you offer size exchanges or returns?
Yes! We accept returns and size exchanges within 14 days of delivery, provided the sneakers are unworn, in pristine condition, and all security tags are fully intact.
Can I request a custom design pair?
Absolutely! You can reach out to our Customization Lab by selecting "Customization Service" in the contact form. Our master artist will contact you to schedule a consultation.
